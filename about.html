About Artavita

Built for artists who don't have a gallery yet

Artavita started as a simple idea: talented artists shouldn't need a gallery relationship to get discovered. Since 2009 we've built a free online gallery where independent artists and small galleries can showcase work, enter juried contests judged by working curators, and win real exhibition opportunities at major art fairs — Artexpo New York, Red Dot Miami, Art Santa Fe, and more.

Today over 12,400 artists across 90 countries use Artavita to build a portfolio, get featured, and connect with collectors and galleries who might never have found them otherwise.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to join?+

Yes — every artist and gallery can create a free profile with up to 5 images, post announcements, and enter contests at no cost. The Featured plan ($6/mo or $69/yr) unlocks unlimited images and homepage placement.

How much does it cost to enter a contest?+

Most contests charge a small per-image entry fee (typically $15) to cover jurying costs. Winners receive a free exhibition slot with no participation fee.

Who judges the contests?+

Contests are juried by working curators and gallery partners affiliated with the fairs where winners exhibit, including Artexpo New York and Red Dot Miami.

Can galleries use Artavita too?+

Yes — galleries can create a free profile, promote exhibitions, and connect with Artavita's global artist community the same way individual artists do.

What's the Artavita Space app?+

Artavita Space is a free companion app (iOS, Android, Mac, and Web) for building and viewing immersive virtual exhibitions.
